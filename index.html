What it is

G.O.S.P.E.L. means good news

Before you walk in, your guide explains the word. Each letter is one part of the story, and each room walks you through it. We'll leave the rest for you to see in person.

  1. G

    God created us to be with him

  2. O

    Our sins separate us from God

  3. S

    Sins cannot be removed by good deeds

  4. P

    Paying the price for sins, Jesus died and rose again

  5. E

    Everyone who trusts in him alone has eternal life

  6. L

    Life with Jesus starts now and lasts forever

Then a quiet room to sit, pray, and talk it over

Questions

Good to know

Is this a church event?

Yes. It's a Christian experience hosted at Harbor Bay, and it's open to anyone, whatever you believe.

Does it cost anything?

No. It's free.

Can I bring my kids or parents?

Yes, all ages are welcome. Families have already signed up together.

Do I have to talk or share anything?

No. You can simply walk through. There's a quiet room afterward if you want to talk.

What's the difference between Sunday and Friday?

On Sundays a guide walks your group through. On select Friday nights, the person who signed up leads their own group.
